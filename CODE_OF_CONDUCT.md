# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct & Sovereign Moderation Standards

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the **SlimefunNewHorizons** organization and the **DrakesCraft Labs** ecosystem pledge to make participation in our community, plugins, and repositories a harassment-free, respectful, and technically rigorous experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## 🛡️ Zero Tolerance Policy on Harassment, Toxicity & Abuse

We maintain an **absolute zero-tolerance policy** towards disrespect, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior:

### Unacceptable Behavior Includes:
* **Direct insults, ad-hominem attacks, name-calling, or derogatory language.**
* **Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.**
* **Toxic entitlement:** demanding instant plugin fixes, insulting the code architecture, or berating maintainers because of server gameplay disputes or changes in upstream APIs.
* **Public or private harassment in issues, discussions, pull requests, or Discord.**
* **Publishing others' private information (doxxing)**, such as physical addresses or identity information.
* **Bad-faith engagement:** opening inflammatory issues, flooding threads with spam or automated low-quality AI comments, or attempting to evade prior blocks.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement & Moderation Protocol

Project maintainers are empowered and obligated to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

When unacceptable behavior is observed, maintainers will enforce the following actions immediately:

1. **Correction / Warning**: Hiding the offensive comment as *abusive* or *off-topic* and issuing a formal notice.
2. **Conversation Lock**: Immediate locking (`Lock conversation as spam / abusive / heated`) of the issue or pull request.
3. **Immediate Account Ban / Block**: Immediate block of the offending GitHub account from all repositories under **SlimefunNewHorizons** without debate.
4. **Escalation**: Serious threats, harassment, or Terms of Service violations will be escalated directly to **GitHub Trust & Safety**.

---

## 📬 Reporting Guidelines

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the lead maintainer:

* **Contact / Moderation:** Open an issue flagged as `[Moderation Report]` or contact `pablo.elias.miranda.292003@gmail.com`.
* All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
